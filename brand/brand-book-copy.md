## 01 Who we are

**Headline:** A distributor built around the bottle.

Liquid Legacy Distribution is a national wine and spirits distributor, established in 2026. We carry a focused portfolio from producers who care how their bottles are represented, and we place those bottles with the retailers, restaurants and bars where they belong.

**Positioning**
> For wineries, distilleries and importers who want their brands sold with care at national scale, Liquid Legacy Distribution is the partner that pairs the reach of a national operator with the attention of a small importer. We keep our portfolio focused and our people accountable to every brand we carry.

---

## 02 Mission, vision, values

**Mission:** To bring every bottle we carry to the right shelf and the right table, in the condition it left the cellar, with its story intact.

**Vision:** To be the distributor producers trust with their legacy, and the one retailers and restaurants are glad to see at the door.

**Values**
- **I. Stewardship.** We handle every brand as if it were our own.
- **II. Reach.** Built to grow market by market without losing the details.
- **III. Accountability.** Clear numbers, straight answers, one team that owns the result.
- **IV. Responsibility.** We market to adults of legal drinking age, and only to them.

---

## 03 Personality and voice

**Personality:** Assured, not loud. Traditional, not dusty. Precise, not cold. Warm, not casual.

**Voice principles**
1. **Say it plainly.** Short sentences, concrete nouns.
2. **Earn trust with specifics.** Never with superlatives.
3. **Respect the craft.** Talk about producers and their work more than about ourselves.
4. **Stay responsible.** Every message is written for adults of legal drinking age.

**We say / We don't say**

| We say | We don't say |
|---|---|
| We place your brand where it belongs. | We'll skyrocket your sales. |
| Clear reporting, every month. | Industry-leading, cutting-edge analytics. |
| Built for national scale. | America's number one distributor. |
| Please enjoy responsibly. | Drink up! |

**Writing rules**
- Sentence case for headlines, buttons and labels.
- No exclamation marks.
- Write "wine and spirits", not "liquor" or "booze".
- Use "Liquid Legacy Distribution" in full on first mention and "Liquid Legacy" after that. "LLD" is for the monogram only, not running copy.
- Publish numbers only when they're verified.

## 04 Logo

### The crest (primary logo)
The crest, with its "Liquid Legacy Distribution" lettering, is the official logo. It's a full-color render and is used **on dark backgrounds only**: the website, presentations, signage and the cover of this book. Parts of it can be used alone: the crest without lettering, the crowned LLD monogram, and the lettering by itself.
- **Minimum size:** 240 px wide on screen, 60 mm in print.
- **Never:** recolor it, place it on light backgrounds, or rebuild its artwork.

### Logo variations (proposed, for approval)
These are flat vector variations drawn from the crest, for places a full-color render can't go: one-color print, foil, embroidery, engraving, small sizes and light backgrounds. They're proposals for approval; until they're approved, the official crest is the only logo in use.

| Mark | Use it for |
|---|---|
| **Horizontal lockup** | Website header, letterhead, email signature, documents. |
| **Wordmark** | Cartons, packaging, places where the monogram already appears nearby. |
| **Monogram** | Favicon, social avatar, embossing, small spaces. |
| **Seal** | Case stamps, wax seals, stickers, merchandise. |
| **Shield** | One-color stand-in for the crest: foil, engraving, uniforms. |

### Clear space
Keep clear space on every side equal to **x**, the cap height of the "L" in the monogram.

### Minimum sizes (screen / print)

| Mark | Screen | Print |
|---|---|---|
| Horizontal lockup | 120 px | 30 mm |
| Wordmark | 140 px | 35 mm |
| Monogram | 24 px | 8 mm |
| Seal | 64 px | 18 mm |
| Shield | 32 px | 10 mm |

### Color versions
- **Gold on obsidian:** primary.
- **Parchment on obsidian or photography:** with a dark scrim.
- **Ink on parchment:** light backgrounds and single-color print.
- **Gold deep on parchment:** premium light applications.

### Misuse
- Don't stretch or squash.
- Don't recolor outside the palette.
- Don't add shadows, glows or bevels to the vector marks.
- Don't rotate.
- Don't place on busy imagery without a scrim.
- Don't retype the wordmark in another typeface.
- Don't place the full crest on a light background.

## 05 Color
Color values: use `brand/tokens.json` exactly.

- **Primary:**
  - Obsidian #12110F
  - Antique Gold #BF9F66
  - Parchment #EFE8DA
- **Secondary:**
  - Coal #1B1A17
  - Char #24221E
  - Gilt #E6D3A3
  - Bronze #755D45
  - Gold Deep #7A5C30
  - Stone #A8A093
  - Ink #1A1815
- **Proportion:** about 70% obsidian and coal, 20% parchment and stone, 10% gold. Gold is an accent, never a background for large areas of text.
- **Accessible pairings** (WCAG contrast):

  | Pairing | Ratio |
  |---|---|
  | Parchment on obsidian | 15.5:1 |
  | Gold on obsidian | 7.5:1 |
  | Stone on obsidian | 7.3:1 |
  | Ink on parchment | 14.5:1 |
  | Gold deep on parchment | 5.1:1 |

  Bronze is decorative only (3.1:1 on obsidian).
- **Print:** CMYK values are in the tokens file. For metallic gold, specify an antique gold foil matched to a physical sample. Pantone references are to be confirmed with the printer.

## 06 Typography
Both families come from American printing. Caslon set the Declaration of Independence; Franklin Gothic is the classic American sans. Both are open source (SIL Open Font License) and free for print and web.

- **Libre Caslon Display:** large headlines only. One weight (Regular); never bold or fake-bold it.
- **Libre Caslon Text:** smaller headings, pull quotes and italics. Regular, Italic and Bold.
- **Libre Franklin:** body copy, navigation, buttons, forms and captions. Regular and Medium.
- **The logo lettering** belongs to the crest artwork and is never retyped.

**Rules**
- Sentence case everywhere: headlines, buttons and labels.
- No spaced-out capitals.

**Hierarchy (size / line height, px)**

| Style | Font | Size / line height |
|---|---|---|
| Display | Libre Caslon Display | 96 / 98 |
| Heading 2 | Libre Caslon Display | 56 / 60 |
| Heading 3 | Libre Caslon Text | 28 / 34 |
| Body | Libre Franklin Regular | 17 / 28 |
| Small | Libre Franklin Medium | 14 / 20 |
| Caption | Libre Franklin | 13 / 20 |

## 07 Photography
**Direction:** real places, real light.
- Environments over people: cellars, warehouses, tasting rooms, back bars, loading docks.
- Natural or practical light, deep shadows, warm amber against cool blue hour.
- Visible texture: oak, stone, glass, steel.

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Natural light | Stock smiles and handshakes |
| Quiet compositions with room for type | Readable third-party labels or logos |
| Honest wear and imperfection | Anyone who could appear under 25 |
| Labels softly out of focus | Over-processed HDR or glossy CGI |
| | Any suggestion of excess or drinking and driving |

---

## 08 Graphic elements
- **The crest** is the hero element. Give it room, and show it on dark stone or obsidian.
- **Gold rules:** 1 px, used to separate items in a list or to frame the crest, not as decoration everywhere.
- **Numbering:** only for real sequences, such as the four steps of how a bottle travels with us.
- **Texture:** dark slate behind the crest, and a fine film grain over photography.
- **The seal as a stamp:** one color, on kraft cartons, wax and paper. This uses the proposed seal variation.

## 09 Applications

- **Business card** (3.5 × 2 in):
  - Front: the seal or monogram in gold on obsidian.
  - Back: name, title and contact in parchment on obsidian, with a small lockup.
  - Sample name: "Name Surname / Title" (clearly a template).
- **Letterhead** (US Letter): horizontal lockup top left, a hairline below it, and a footer line for the address.
- **Email signature:** name, title, phone and email, with the lockup at 160 px wide.
- **Case stamp:** the seal in one color on a kraft carton.
- **Delivery vehicle:** a white box truck in side view.
  - Horizontal lockup in obsidian on the box side.
  - Seal on the rear doors.
  - A gold-deep hairline along the base.
- **Apparel:** an embroidered monogram patch on a dark work shirt.
- **Website:** real screenshots of the live landing page (desktop and mobile).
