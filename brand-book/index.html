    01
    Who we are

    A distributor built around the bottle.

    Liquid Legacy Distribution is a national wine and spirits distributor, established in 2026. We carry a focused portfolio from producers who care how their bottles are represented, and we place those bottles with the retailers, restaurants and bars where they belong.

    Positioning

    For wineries, distilleries and importers who want their brands sold with care at national scale, Liquid Legacy Distribution is the partner that pairs the reach of a national operator with the attention of a small importer. We keep our portfolio focused and our people accountable to every brand we carry.

    02

    Mission, vision, values

    Mission

    To bring every bottle we carry to the right shelf and the right table, in the condition it left the cellar, with its story intact.

    Vision

    To be the distributor producers trust with their legacy, and the one retailers and restaurants are glad to see at the door.

    Values

    • Stewardship

      We handle every brand as if it were our own.

    • Reach

      Built to grow market by market without losing the details.

    • Accountability

      Clear numbers, straight answers, one team that owns the result.

    • Responsibility

      We market to adults of legal drinking age, and only to them.

    03

    Personality and voice

    Assured, not loud.
    Traditional, not dusty.
    Precise, not cold.
    Warm, not casual.

    Voice principles

    • Say it plainly.

      Short sentences, concrete nouns.

    • Earn trust with specifics.

      Never with superlatives.

    • Respect the craft.

      Talk about producers and their work more than about ourselves.

    • Stay responsible.

      Every message is written for adults of legal drinking age.

    How we write

    We sayWe don't say
    We place your brand where it belongs.We'll skyrocket your sales.
    Clear reporting, every month.Industry-leading, cutting-edge analytics.
    Built for national scale.America's number one distributor.
    Please enjoy responsibly.Drink up!

    Writing rules

    • Sentence case for headlines, buttons and labels.
    • No exclamation marks.
    • Write "wine and spirits", not "liquor" or "booze".
    • Use "Liquid Legacy Distribution" in full on first mention and "Liquid Legacy" after that. "LLD" is for the monogram only, not running copy.
    • Publish numbers only when they're verified.
    04
    Logo

    The crest

    Primary logo

    The crest, with its "Liquid Legacy Distribution" lettering, is the official logo. It's a full-color render and is used on dark backgrounds only: the website, presentations, signage and the cover of this book.

    Parts of it can be used alone: the crest without lettering, the crowned LLD monogram, and the lettering by itself.

    Minimum size240 px wide on screen, 60 mm in print
    NeverRecolor it, place it on light backgrounds, or rebuild its artwork.

    Parts of the crest

    Logo variations

    Proposed, for approval

    These are flat vector variations drawn from the crest, for places a full-color render can't go: one-color print, foil, embroidery, engraving, small sizes and light backgrounds. They are proposals for approval; until they're approved, the official crest is the only logo in use.

    Horizontal lockupWebsite header, letterhead, email signature, documents.
    WordmarkCartons, packaging, places where the monogram already appears nearby.
    MonogramFavicon, social avatar, embossing, small spaces.
    SealCase stamps, wax seals, stickers, merchandise.
    ShieldOne-color stand-in for the crest: foil, engraving, uniforms.

    Clear space

    Keep clear space on every side equal to x, the cap height of the "L" in the monogram.

    Color versions

    Gold on obsidian. Primary.

    Parchment on obsidian or photography. With a dark scrim.

    Misuse

    The marks are drawn once and used as supplied. These are the mistakes to catch before anything goes to print.

    Don't stretch or squash.

    Don't recolor outside the palette.

    Don't add shadows, glows or bevels to the vector marks.

    Don't rotate.

    Don't place on busy imagery without a scrim.

    05

    Color

    Three primary colors carry the brand. The secondary colors are for surfaces, text and fine detail.

    Proportion

    About 70% obsidian and coal, 20% parchment and stone, 10% gold. Gold is an accent, never a background for large areas of text.

    Obsidian and coal, 70%
    Parchment and stone, 20%
    Gold, 10%

    Accessible pairings

    WCAG contrast ratios for text. Bronze is decorative only (3.1:1 on obsidian).

    Print: CMYK values are in the tokens file. For metallic gold, specify an antique gold foil matched to a physical sample. Pantone references are to be confirmed with the printer.

    Typography

    Both families come from American printing. Caslon set the Declaration of Independence; Franklin Gothic is the classic American sans. Both are open source (SIL Open Font License) and free for print and web.

    Aa
    Libre Caslon Display

    Large headlines only. One weight (Regular); never bold or fake-bold it.

    ABCDEFGHIJKLM
    NOPQRSTUVWXYZ
    abcdefghijklm
    nopqrstuvwxyz
    0123456789

    Aa
    Libre Caslon Text

    Smaller headings, pull quotes and italics. Regular, Italic and Bold.

    Regular for headings. Italic for quotes and asides. Bold, sparingly.

    Aa
    Libre Franklin

    Body copy, navigation, buttons, forms and captions. Regular and Medium.

    Regular sets running text at a comfortable measure. Medium is for buttons and small labels.

    07
    Photography

    Real places, real light.

    • Environments over people: cellars, warehouses, tasting rooms, back bars, loading docks.
    • Natural or practical light, deep shadows, warm amber against cool blue hour.
    • Visible texture: oak, stone, glass, steel.

    Don't

    • Stock smiles and handshakes
    • Readable third-party labels or logos
    • Anyone who could appear under 25
    • Over-processed HDR or glossy CGI
    • Any suggestion of excess or drinking and driving
